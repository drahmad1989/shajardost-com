2. Our Services

  • Tree donations and sponsorship: you contribute money, we plant and care for trees under our programs (Mission 50, Miyawaki forests, community plantations).

  • Plantation services: sponsored plantations for homes, schools, farms and businesses.
  • Programs and training: Green Scouts school sessions, volunteer activities and awareness events.

3. Donations, Prices and Payments

Rates for tree donations, sponsorships and plants are as published on the website or as quoted to you directly (by phone or chat) at the time of your order. Payments are made through the bank transfer or mobile wallet details we provide. Please note:

  • Work (planting/care) begins once payment is confirmed.
  • Quoted rates may be revised from time to time; the rate confirmed to you at the time of your order applies to your order.
  • Donations are used for saplings, planting, water, care, guarding and replacement during the committed care period.

4. Donor Trees, Care and Certificates

Trees are living things — their growth depends on soil, water and weather. We follow a scheduled care plan (watering, protection and replacement of failed saplings) for the committed care period, and we care about every tree's survival. Where a certificate or GPS location was promised for your donation, we provide it after plantation is completed, normally within the timeframe told to you at the time of donation. If a promised item is delayed, contact us and we will make it right.

5. Plant Orders

Plant orders are confirmed by phone or chat. Delivery timelines are agreed at order time and may vary with season and distance. If a plant arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we will arrange a replacement or fair solution. Risk passes to you at handover for collected orders, or at delivery for delivered orders.
