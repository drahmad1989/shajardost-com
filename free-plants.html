How It Works

Four Simple Steps

1

Post a request

Fill in the form — which plants, how many, and for what purpose. Your number stays with the team only — never public.

2

We verify it

Our team calls you within 24–48 hours to verify the request. After approval it goes live on the wall (with the estimated cost).

3

Anyone can sponsor

Any person or company can see a request on the wall and sponsor it — by call or on DeraTalk. Full or partial — both work.

4

We plant

The ShajarDost team buys the plants and plants them itself — the card is updated to "Fulfilled ✓" with a photo on the wall.
