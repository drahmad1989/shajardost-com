Free Plants — Mang Bhejo, Poora Ho Jayegi

How It Works

Char Aasan Steps

1

Mang bhejein

Form bharein — kaun se pouday, kitne, kis maqsad ke liye. Number sirf team ke paas — public kabhi nahi.

2

Team verify kare

Team 24–48 ghantay mein call kar ke mang verify karti hai. Approval ke baad mang wall par aati hai (taqreeban kharcha ke sath).

3

Koi bhi sponser ban jaye

Koi bhi banda ya company mang dekh kar sponser ban sakta hai — call ya DeraTalk par. Poora ya aadha, dono chalta hai.

4

Team plant kare

ShajarDost team pouday khud khareed kar khud lagati hai — photo ke sath "Poora hua ✓" wall par aa jata hai.
