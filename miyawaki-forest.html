About Miyawaki Forest

Japanese method of planting dense forests that grow 10x faster and become 30x denser than conventional plantations. Mature in 3 years.

Key Features

  • 10x faster growth
  • 30x more density
  • Specialized native species
  • Soil preparation included
  • 1 year maintenance
  • Matures in 3 years (not 20)
  • 30x more oxygen output
  • Self-sustaining ecosystem
