The honesty part (please read)

  • We do not sell "planted" counts. A tree counts for us when it is alive at year three — care is in the price, not an add-on.
  • We do not promise 100% survival. Nature does not work that way; our ledger even carries a replacements line, and our reports tell you what died and what we replanted.
  • We provide receipts and documentation for your CSR records — we do not give tax advice, and we do not make tax-benefit claims.
  • One-day event plantations without aftercare are the #1 killer of saplings. We will say this to your face, because our published myths article says it to everyone else.

Plantation Day

How an Employee Drive Works

Half a day at our Kasur-district sites (about an hour from Lahore). Our team runs the ground; your team plants, learns and photographs everything.

01

Before

Date, participant count and site confirmed. Saplings (native species), pits and tools prepped by our team. Safety briefing sent to your HR.

02

The Day

Welcome + 15-minute method briefing. Teams plant with our guidance — auger on site, species explained, mulch demonstration. Group photos at your grove.

03

After

Our crew waters and protects the site through the 3-year establishment period. You receive the day's photos, GPS points and the species list.

04

Follow-ups

Survival reports at 6 and 12 months with photos; the 3-year establishment completion report closes the promise.

FAQ

Questions Companies Ask

Does our company need to provide land?

No. By default we plant on our own care sites in Kasur district and on partner schools' grounds. If your company has its own land, we can plant there instead — dense Miyawaki-style clusters work especially well for campus boundaries.

What does Rs.3,200 per tree include?

The native sapling, pit digging, soil preparation and planting, plus three years of establishment care — watering, watchman protection, fertilizer and replacements — and your reporting package. The rate matches our published care ledger (Rs.802 per tree per year plus Rs.150 one-time planting) and our public donate page.

Can our employees join a planting day?

Yes. We host half-day plantation drives at our sites: saplings, tools, an auger and on-ground guidance are provided by our team; the company arranges its own transport to the site. Drives work best with 15–60 participants.

What reporting will we receive?

Planting-day photos, GPS points and the species list; survival-check reports at 6 and 12 months; and a three-year establishment completion report. All figures come from the same ledger we publish publicly.

Will our brand be visible?

Where the site agreement allows, a plantation board carries your company's name, and you receive a partnership certificate, a listing on our partners section, and social media features as the grove establishes.

Do you do one-off event plantations?

Yes, but we recommend the three-year bundles. A one-day event without aftercare is the single biggest cause of sapling deaths — the uncared-for half of our published survival data. If budget only allows an event, we say so honestly and price the care anyway, or suggest starting with a Grove 50.
