Volunteer With Us

Plant, protect and grow Pakistan's green future. Whether you have 2 hours a week or one day a month — your time plants a legacy.

Why Volunteer With ShajarDost?

ShajarDost is a community movement that has planted over 1.2 million trees across Punjab — including the national record of 630,000 trees in a single day. Volunteers are the heart of this movement: they plant, water, guard, teach and inspire. When you volunteer with us, you don't just plant a tree — you learn how a forest is built, meet people who care, and see your effort grow year after year.

What You Get

  • Hands-on plantation and nursery training
  • Volunteer certificate and public recognition
  • Real field experience in Miyawaki and mass plantation projects
  • Community of green-minded friends near you
  • Student volunteers: activity letters for school/college requirements
Find Your Role

Ways You Can Help

No experience needed — we train you on site. Pick what matches your interest.

Tree Planting

Join plantation drives — dig, plant, mulch. Great for groups, families and first-timers.

Nursery Care

Water, pot and protect saplings at our nursery. Weekly slots available.

Photography & Video

Capture plantation drives and tree growth stories for our YouTube and gallery.

Content Writing

Write tree-care guides, impact stories and social posts in English or Urdu.

Social Media

Help us reach more donors and volunteers by managing posts and replies.

Teaching (Green Scouts)

Teach school students about native trees and plantation during Green Scouts sessions.

Event Management

Organize drives, coordinate teams and manage logistics on plantation days.

Driving & Logistics

Help transport saplings, tools and volunteers to plantation sites.
