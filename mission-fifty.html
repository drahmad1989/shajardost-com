Mission Fifty

Plant 50. Care for 50. Document your journey. Share it with #ShajarDost #MissionFifty — verified journeys get featured on our official channels.

About Mission Fifty

Mission Fifty is our flagship public movement: every person plants at least 50 trees in their lifetime — at home, in school, on the farm, or in a permitted community space — and cares for them. You document your journey in videos and share them with #ShajarDost and #MissionFifty. ShajarDost reviews each submission and features verified journeys on our official channels to inspire the next planter.

The Movement

  • Free to join — no fee, just a promise to nature
  • Plant anywhere permitted — garden, school, farm, community space
  • Journey milestones: 5, 10, 25 and 50 plants
  • Planting videos, progress updates and milestone videos
  • Verified submissions featured on official ShajarDost channels
  • Mission Fifty completion certificate after evidence review

How Mission Fifty Works

Five steps — from your first plant to your 50-tree milestone.

1

Plant

At home, school, farm or a permitted community space. Take permission where needed.

2

Water and protect your plants — planting is the start; survival comes from care.

Celebrate

Mission Fifty 🏁

After evidence review, your completion is recognised with a Mission Fifty certificate.

Honest counting: a planting record counts plants put in the ground; a surviving-tree count is separate. Certificates recognise verified planting milestones — they never claim a number of surviving trees. Trees planted earlier can be included if you have credible records; otherwise label them as historical.

Video Guide

What to Record & Share

Three simple video types. Include the date, your city, and roughly how many plants — no fancy editing needed.

Planting Video

While planting or right after — show the plants, the spot and the date.

Progress Video

Weeks or months later — care and growth updates. These matter most.

Milestone Video

When you reach 10, 25 or 50 recorded plants — celebrate your journey.

Privacy first: get permission before filming identifiable people — especially children — and never share your exact home address. Featuring is not guaranteed: ShajarDost reviews submissions and publishes selected journeys with your permission.
