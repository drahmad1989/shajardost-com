How It Works

Teen Aasan Steps

1

Demand bhejein

Neeche wala form bharein — kaun se pouday, kitne, kis maqsad ke liye. Number sirf team ke paas.

2

Admin approve kare

Hamari team 24–48 ghantay mein aap se rabta karti hai. Approval ke baad entry wall par aati hai.

3

Pouday milte hain

Donor ya ShajarDost ki team demand poora karti hai — status wall par "Poora hua" ho jata hai.
