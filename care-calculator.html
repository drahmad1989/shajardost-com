🌳 ShajarDost — Care Cost Calculator

1 tree ka saalana care cost naapein → rates final karein. Neeche EXAMPLE values dali hain — apne ASAL hisaab se badlein. Har tabdeeli par foran nateeja update ho jayega.

Naapne wali 5 cheezein (field par likh lein)

  1. Paani: mahine ka average kharcha — tanker kitne mein aata hai / motor-diesel-kitna? (garmi aur sardi ka average lein)
  2. Chowkidar/mali: mahine ki tankhwah (agar site share hai to hissa lein, poora nahi)
  3. Bijli/diesel: pump/motor ka mahana kharcha
  4. Khaad/dawa + auzaar/fencing: saal mein kitna gaya
  5. Replacement: 100 mein se kitne pouday murjha jate hain (1 saal mein)? aur naya pouday lagane ka kharcha kitna?

💡 Tip: purane 3 mahine ke paise wale register/receipts dekh kar 3-mahina average × 4 = saalana nikal lein. Ye sab se sahi tareeqa hai.
