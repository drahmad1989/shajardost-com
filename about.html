About ShajarDost

A movement dedicated to making Pakistan greener, one tree at a time.

Our Story

ShajarDost began as a vision to make Pakistan greener, one tree at a time. We are proud contributors to the Ten Billion Trees Project, a national initiative to combat climate change and deforestation.

On 14th August 2018, we set a national record by planting 630,000 trees in a single day across Punjab. Since then, we've planted over 1.2 million trees through our community of volunteers, students, and donors.

Our programs — Mission 50, Green Scouts, Mini Nursery, and Miyawaki Forest — give every Pakistani a way to contribute to a greener future, regardless of whether they have land, money, or just time to give.
